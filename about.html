About Vigil

A personal discipline log. Built by one person, for the phone in his pocket, and left on that phone.

AC

Aaron Celestian

Mineral scientist. Curator of Mineral Sciences at the Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County. The person who writes this app, and uses it.

Why I made this

I built Vigil because I needed a place to keep a standard, and I did not want that place to belong to a company.

My work is a long watch. I study how minerals remember the worlds that made them — salt crystals that still hold ancient water, frameworks that pull poison out of a river, records that take years to read. Science rewards the people who show up again tomorrow. A life is the same problem, with worse instrumentation. I forget the days I did not train. I negotiate with myself at night. I mean to call someone and then the week is gone.

I wanted a log that treated a day the way a lab notebook treats an experiment: dated, honest, and mine. Four numbers that tell the truth. The body. The mind. The people. The standard you keep when nobody is watching.

Most discipline apps asked for an account, a feed, and a subscription before they would let me check a box. I did not want a public score. I did not want my habits on someone else’s server. I did not want to be sold a new identity every morning. I wanted the quiet version: do the work, mark it, see the climb, promise yourself something rare when the rank actually changes.

So I made this for my own iPhone. If you are using it too, welcome. The log stays here. There is no account and no audience. Ranks, streaks, and the rewards you name for yourself are only a way to remember why tomorrow still matters.

Keep the watch.

What Vigil is

Daily habits earn XP. XP raises Physical, Mental, Social, and Discipline, and a rank that gets harder to reach. Focus, sleep, workouts, and a short journal count. You can name a reward for a future level, and Vigil will remind you how close you are.

Nothing here is uploaded. There is no login. A backup leaves this device only if you share or copy it.
