Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026. This page is the policy for Vigil, a personal web app by Aaron Celestian.

Vigil does not have an account, a company server, or analytics. What you type stays in this browser on this device unless you export it yourself.

Who is responsible

Vigil is a personal project built and maintained by Aaron Celestian (“I,” “me”). It is not operated by the Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County, USC, NASA, or JPL.

Questions: aaron.celestian@gmail.com.

What this app stores on your device

Vigil is a page you open in a browser or pin to the home screen. It writes a private log in that browser’s storage for this site, including:

  • The name and archetype you choose
  • Habits, the days you complete them, and XP
  • Focus minutes, sleep, workouts, journal entries, and the scratch list
  • Daily challenges and the rewards you promise yourself
  • Theme, sound, and the time of your last backup
  • An in-progress focus timer, kept only for the current browser session

That data never leaves the device on its own. I cannot see it. I do not receive a copy.

What I do not collect

  • No account, email signup, or login
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking pixels
  • No cookies used to identify you across sites
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health-app access
  • No third-party fonts, ads, or social SDKs

Opening Vigil does not send your log to me. If this page is hosted on the public web, your browser may still make the ordinary request for the app files (HTML, CSS, icons, and the service worker). Those requests can appear in the host’s server logs the way any website visit does. They are not your habit history.

Backups you choose to make

On the You tab you can share, copy, or download a JSON backup, and you can restore one. That file contains your log. It leaves the phone only because you sent it — to Files, to another device, to a friend, or to a chat. Treat it like a private notebook. Anyone who has the file can restore it in Vigil.

A backup is also how you move the log to a new phone. Different addresses (for example a files-app copy versus a site on Wi-Fi) are separate logs. Pinning the same URL to the home screen keeps using that site’s log.

Device features you can allow

  • Notifications. Optional. If you allow them, Vigil can show a local “focus block done” notice. It is created on the device. It is not sent through my servers.
  • Screen wake lock. Used only while a focus timer is running, so the screen is less likely to sleep.
  • Home screen / offline shell. A service worker may cache the app files on this device so Vigil still opens without a network. The cache is the app, not a copy of your log on a server.

You can revoke notification permission in the phone’s settings. Clearing this site’s data, or using Start over, deletes the local log on this origin.

Children

Vigil is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children. The log is whatever the person using this device types.

Your rights

Because I do not receive your log, there is no profile of you on my side to access, correct, export, or delete. You already hold the data:

  • Access and edit it in the app
  • Export it with Share backup or Copy backup
  • Delete it with Start over, or by clearing this site’s data in the browser, or by deleting the home-screen app and its site data

I do not sell personal information. I do not share it for advertising. California CCPA and European GDPR rights still matter in principle; in practice there is no Vigil database of users to make a request against. If you write to me, I can answer questions about this policy. I cannot retrieve a log I never received.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date on this page. The current version is the one published here.
